# User research 

## Target audience
For my audience I am interested in reaching people who listen to mainstream media. For example, people I am looking for could be the average radio listener, someone who pays attention to pop culture, someone who watches the grammys. I am looking for people who are in their early twenties to forties as I am making time comparisons in my visuals, which I believe will be more relevant to that age demographic since they will be more familiar with the artists in my dataset.  

## Interview script
> List the goals from your research, and the questions you intend to ask. 
Goals:
- Assess the likelihood of a grammy winning “best new artist” will achieve commercial success. 
- Assess how popular were best new artist winners prior to winning the Grammy and after winning.
- Are there trends in consistency of commercial success or the amount of songs or albums that become hits.

Interview Questions:
1. How could I improve my career timeline sketch to make the message clearer to viewers?
2. Should I null the artist who doesn't have any hits or leave it to reflect every year? 
3. How many sketches should I focus on in the presentation and which were the most impactful? 
4. Is showing hits versus albums relevant? Should I get rid of the inner circles in the bubble chart that have the album names? 
5. Is the story clear with the order of my sketches? 
  
## Interview findings
> Detail the findings from your interviews.  Do not include PII.  Capture specific insights where possible.
1. Interview 1 said that my idea was clear. Interview 2 said it could be helpful for the shorter years to have color. They suggested a gradient with the lighter color being the shorter amount of time. Interview 2 that the timeline could be staggered by year so the timeline aspect of the story becomes clearer to my audience. 
2. Interview 1 said I should get rid of the artists who had 0 hits. Interview 2 agreed and said it would not disrupt the timeline overall. Interview 3 suggested that I include the artists name and that datawrapper would be easier to edit for this specific visual than the Tableau one I had made.
3. Interview 1 said that I am going to be talking a lot more due to the amount of data so they said 3. Interview 2 said 4 because they wanted to see the before and after an artist won a Grammy. Interview 3 said that the timeline would be the most important piece and the top songs/albums would complement it.
4. Interview 1 said that they could see arguments for keeping or getting rid of the circles. Interview 2 said I should get rid of the inner circles as it could be confusing. Interview 3 disagreed but thought it might be better to come up with different versions for the presentation to make it less complex. Interview 3 said that the inner circles would read better on a phone/mobile device because they can click on them to get more information (like hovering in tableau).
5. Interview 1 said that the timeline should be first and the total songs and albums should be last. Interview two said it should be the before and after chart, then timeline, and finally the total songs and albums. Interview 3 said to do the timeline first the total songs and albums.
